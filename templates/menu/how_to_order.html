Four steps

How to order

Ordering is available to dine-in guests only, so the first step is simply to take a seat.

  1. Take a seat

    Find any free table. Every table has its own QR code printed on the table card.

  2. Scan the QR code on your table

    Point your phone camera at the code and open the link. The menu opens for your table only, so your order always reaches the right seat.

  3. Browse the menu and build your order

    Tap an item to add it to your cart, set the quantity, and add a note if you want something left off or on the side.

  4. Send it to the kitchen

    Check your cart, confirm, and submit. You will see your order status update on screen while the kitchen cooks it fresh.

Your menu session

Each scan opens the menu for a limited time. If it closes before you are finished, just scan the table code again.

Special requests

Allergy or a change to an item? Add it to the note on the item so the kitchen sees it with your order.

Need a hand?

Any of our staff can walk you through the scan and place the order with you.
