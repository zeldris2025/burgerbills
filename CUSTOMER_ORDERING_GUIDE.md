# 🍔 Burger Bills - Customer Ordering Guide

## How to Order - Step by Step

### **Step 1: Scan the QR Code** 📱
- Find the **QR code on your table**
- Open your phone camera or QR code scanner app
- **Point at the QR code** until it opens automatically
- Wait for the menu to load

---

### **Step 2: Browse the Menu** 👀
- Swipe through **categories** at the top (Burgers, Drinks, Sides, Desserts)
- Tap on a category to see items
- Each item shows:
  - 🖼️ Picture of the food
  - 📝 Description
  - 💰 Price
  - 🌱 Dietary tags (Vegetarian, Spicy, etc.)

---

### **Step 3: Add Items to Cart** ➕
- Find an item you want
- Tap the **"Add"** button
- A quick order panel appears on the side/bottom showing your items
- **Add more items** by tapping "Add" on other menu items
- You can add the **same item multiple times**

---

### **Step 4: Review Your Order** 📋
- Look at the **quick order panel** showing:
  - Items you've added
  - Number of each item
  - Total price
- Want to change quantities? See Step 5
- Want to proceed? Go to Step 6

---

### **Step 5: (Optional) Add Special Requests** 📝
- Tap **"View Full Order"** button
- For each item, you can add:
  - "No onions"
  - "Extra cheese"
  - "Well done"
  - Any other special instructions
- Tap back when done

---

### **Step 6: Submit Your Order** ✅
- Tap **"Proceed to Checkout"** button
- Review your order one final time
- Tap **"Confirm Order"**
- **Wait for confirmation** - a receipt will appear
- A staff member will prepare your food!

---

### **Step 7: Track Your Order** 🕐
- After ordering, the page shows:
  - **Order number** (write it down!)
  - **Estimated time** (e.g., "20 minutes")
  - **Status updates** (Preparing → Ready → Complete)
- **Refresh the page** to see latest status
- **Listen for announcements** from staff

---

## ⏱️ Important: Session Timeout

⚠️ **Your menu access expires after 1 hour**

- If the menu stops working, **scan the QR code again**
- This takes only 2 seconds
- Gives you a fresh 1-hour session

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I edit my order after submitting?**
- A: No, once submitted it goes to the kitchen. If needed, ask a staff member.

**Q: How long does food take?**
- A: Usually 15-25 minutes. Check the "Estimated time" shown.

**Q: Do I need WiFi?**
- A: No! The menu works on cellular data.

**Q: What if I accidentally ordered wrong?**
- A: Immediately tell a staff member - they can help!

**Q: How do I pay?**
- A: A staff member will bring the bill with your order.

---

## 🆘 Need Help?

- 🙋 **Ask a staff member** at the counter
- 📍 **Look for the "Help" button** on menu (if available)
- 🔄 **Rescan the QR code** if menu stops working
